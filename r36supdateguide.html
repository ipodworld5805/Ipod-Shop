R36S Update Guide

Use this guide to back up your SD card and update your R36S safely.

Back to Update Guides Need Help?

What You Need

  • MicroSD card (the one in your R36S)
  • MicroSD card reader (USB)
  • A Windows PC, Mac, or Linux computer
  • 10–30 minutes of time

Step 1: Back Up Your SD Card

  1. Power off the R36S completely.
  2. Remove the MicroSD card and insert it into your computer.
  3. Create a folder named R36S Backup on your computer.
  4. Copy all files from the SD card into that folder.

Tip: If your update goes wrong, this backup saves you.

Step 2: Get the Update Files

  • Download the correct firmware/update package for your R36S model.
Download Update Files

Step 3: Install the Update

  1. Extract the downloaded zip file on your computer (if it’s zipped).
  2. Follow the update package instructions (some updates require flashing an SD image).
  3. When finished, safely eject the SD card from your computer.
  4. Insert the SD card back into the R36S and power it on.

Important: Do not remove power while the device is updating.

Troubleshooting

  • Device won’t boot: Re-seat the SD card and try again.
  • Black screen: Restore from your backup or re-flash the SD image.
  • Corrupted SD card: Try a different SD card (quality brand recommended).
